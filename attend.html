Getting to Rabat

Rabat is reachable through several of Morocco's international airports:

  • Rabat-Sale Airport is the closest and most convenient, with bus and taxi connections into the city.
  • Casablanca Mohammed V Airport is Morocco's main hub, with the widest choice of international connections and a direct train to Rabat.
  • Tangier Ibn Battuta Airport connects to Rabat by the Al Boraq high-speed train.
  • Marrakech Menara Airport is another international gateway, with train connections to Rabat.

From Rabat-Sale Airport into the city

  • Airport bus: runs every 15 minutes to central Rabat, 25 MAD per passenger, card payment accepted.
  • Official taxis: available outside the terminal, with a transport kiosk in the public hall to arrange the journey. Around 150 MAD to central Rabat during the day and 200 MAD at night.
  • inDrive: widely used in Rabat and convenient for going straight to your hotel or to the venue.

Getting around Rabat

  • Blue taxis are for short trips inside the city. They are metered, with a minimum fare of 7 MAD, and can be hailed on the street.
  • White taxis connect Rabat with Sale, Temara and Skhirat. They are generally shared, and fares run from 5 to 15 MAD depending on the destination.
  • The Rabat-Sale tramway serves Agdal, Hassan, the city centre, the Medina and Sale. A ticket costs 7 MAD and can also be bought through the Tram Mobile app.
  • Ride-hailing apps (inDrive, Yango, Uber) operate across Rabat. Payment is in cash.

Exploring Rabat

Rabat is a UNESCO World Heritage city and compact enough to see on foot between sessions. The guide above covers these in more detail:

  • Hassan Tower and the Mohammed V Mausoleum
  • The Kasbah of the Oudayas and the Andalusian Garden
  • The Chellah archaeological site
  • The old Medina
  • The Bouregreg marina
  • Rabat beach, a popular surf spot

Visa

Visa requirements for Morocco depend on your nationality. Detailed guidance will be published here. If you need a letter of invitation in support of a visa application, please write to organizers-2027@eurosys.org and allow as much time as possible before travelling.
